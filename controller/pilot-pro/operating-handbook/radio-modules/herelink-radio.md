# Herelink Radio

## Binding

* Prepare tweezers or paperclip.

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Astro and Alta X Gen 2" %}
- Power on Astro or Alta X Gen 2 with one battery
-   Find the bind button on the IO Panel - next to the yellow XT connector.

    <figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (26).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
- Press on the bind button 3 times. The boom LED starts to blink white confirming that it is in binding mode.

> The boom LEDs blink during pairing only on drone firmware **v2.3.7 or later**.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_0764.gif" alt="" width="360"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Alta X Gen 1" %}
* Using tweezers, press and hold the Herelink unit's "Pair/Reset" button until LED2 blinks (hold approximately 3 seconds)
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}

## Pilot Pro

*   Using tweezers, press and hold the Pilot Pro Herelink unit's "Pair/Reset" button until LED2 blinks (hold approximately 3 seconds)<br>

    <figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/herelink_pair.JPG" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Ensure the light goes solid and the Boom LEDs on the Drone go back to its original colors.
* Open the flight app on Pilot Pro (AMC for Astro) and verify the connection to the aircraft
